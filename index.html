Dig Site

Passthrough AR for Meta Quest 3: a little dig site on your table, with an excavator to drive and dig with, its operator, a worker who keeps out of the way, and rocks and earth to move about.

Together

Two headsets at one table can share the dig site: one starts a room (Share on the in-AR menu, or the button below), the other joins it with the room code. Whoever is pushing drives; both see the other's head and hands.

Before you start

  1. Your room scan needs a table. In the headset: Settings › Physical space › Space setup. Quest 3 usually finds tables by itself; if not, add one and label it Table.
  2. When the browser asks about spatial data, choose Allow. The app reads your room setup to find the table. If it also asks about hand tracking, allow it to use pinch; controllers work either way.

In AR

  • The dig site lands on the nearest table (preferring one in front of you), facing you. If it's out of view, the panel says which way to look. Each table keeps its own dig site, and it's all remembered for next time.
  • Several tables? Pull the trigger or pinch to move to the next one. No table found? Pull the trigger or pinch to open Space Setup.
  • Left thumbstick drives the excavator, forwards and backwards along the way it faces; right thumbstick swings the cab. Hold A to dig: a scoop fills the bucket with earth and picks up a rock, and the dump drops them under the bucket. Hold B to repaint the excavator.
  • A small menu floats above your left controller (or left hand): point at it with the right one and pull the trigger for Exit AR, Reload, Sound, Steering (relative or absolute), Reset, Look, Share and Align. Squeeze a grip to wave at the others in a shared room.
