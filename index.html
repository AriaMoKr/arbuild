Table Cube

Passthrough AR for Meta Quest 3: puts a cube at the centre of your table.

Checking for WebXR support…

Before you start

  1. Your room scan needs a table. In the headset: Settings › Physical space › Space setup. Quest 3 usually finds tables by itself; if not, add one and label it Table.
  2. When the browser asks about spatial data, choose Allow. The app reads your room setup to find the table. If it also asks about hand tracking, allow it to use pinch; controllers work either way.

In AR

  • The cube lands on the nearest table in front of you. If it's elsewhere, the panel says which way to look.
  • Several tables? Pull the trigger or pinch to move the cube to the next one.
  • No table found? Pull the trigger or pinch to open Space Setup.
